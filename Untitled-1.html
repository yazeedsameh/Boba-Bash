Basic Parts of a Car Engine

I made this website to learn more about car engines. An engine changes energy from fuel into movement. Here are some of its main parts.

Engine Parts

What are the main parts?

1. Piston

The piston moves up and down inside the cylinder. This movement helps the engine produce power.

2. Crankshaft

The crankshaft changes the piston's movement into rotation.

3. Spark Plug

In a petrol engine, the spark plug creates a spark to ignite the fuel and air mixture.

4. Cylinder

The cylinder contains the piston and is where combustion takes place in a typical petrol engine.

5. Camshaft

The camshaft helps open and close the engine valves at the right time.

6. Valves

Valves let the air-fuel mixture enter and allow exhaust gases to leave the cylinder.
